Legal information

Legal texts of Ofimeo.

  • Legal notice

    This legal notice governs access to and use of the Ofimeo website and application, in accordance with Article 10 of Spanish Law 34/2002 of 11 July on information society services and electronic commerce (LSSI-CE).

  • Privacy policy

    Ofimeo is designed so that its owner does not receive your documents or your data: everything is stored on your device and exchanged, encrypted, directly with the people you share with. This policy explains what data exist, where they are and who may process them, in accordance with Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD).

  • Cookies and local storage policy

    Ofimeo does not use cookies or any tracking, analytics or advertising technology. It only stores in your browser the information strictly necessary for the application to work, and that information is not sent to the owner.

  • Terms of use

    These terms govern the use of Ofimeo, a free collaborative office suite that runs in the browser and is designed especially for schools. By using it you accept these terms and the Legal notice.

  • Accessibility statement

    Pablo Gallego Falcón is committed to making Ofimeo accessible in accordance with Spanish Royal Decree 1112/2018 of 7 September on the accessibility of public sector websites and mobile applications, which transposes Directive (EU) 2016/2102.

  • Information for schools

    A guide for school leadership teams, ICT coordinators and data protection officers (DPOs) of schools and education authorities that want to use Ofimeo: what data are processed, where they are, who is responsible, what the risks are and how to configure it. It includes a model entry for the record of processing activities and an information text for families.

  • Note on artificial intelligence

    Ofimeo does not include any artificial intelligence systems. For transparency and for the purposes of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act), this note explains which features might be mistaken for AI and how the optional connection with external assistants via WebMCP works.
